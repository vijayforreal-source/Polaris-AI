# Verified Environmental Forcing Along A76C

Constant velocity averaged 65.583 km error in the Checkpoint 5 rolling evaluation and therefore
does not describe A76C adequately. This checkpoint aligns environmental fields with the observed
track without implementing a trajectory predictor.

## Classification semantics

- `OBSERVATION`: measured or remotely sensed source data, including USNIC positions and OSI-SAF
  sea-ice concentration.
- `ANALYSIS`: a numerical-model state constrained by an operational analysis system. The Mercator
  GLO12 current fields use this classification.
- `REANALYSIS`: a retrospective, consistently processed model/data-assimilation reconstruction.
  ERA5 uses this classification.
- `FORECAST`: a source prediction valid after its initialization.
- `MODEL_PREDICTION`: output generated by a POLARIS-AI model or baseline.

Existing serialized values remain unchanged.

## Ocean currents

The live Copernicus Marine catalogue verified product
`GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024`, dataset
`cmems_mod_glo_phy-cur_anfc_0.083deg_PT6H-i`, version `202406`. It is a Mercator GLO12 Level-4
numerical-model product on a regular approximately 1/12-degree latitude/longitude grid. `uo` is
eastward and `vo` northward sea-water velocity; both have provider units `m s-1`. The files contain
6-hourly instantaneous fields.

A76C spans longitude -38.50 to -29.50 and latitude -60.53 to -52.08. A configurable 1-degree
buffer produced the requested envelope -39.50 to -28.50 and -61.53 to -51.08 without longitude
wrap. Three exact source levels were selected: 0.494025 m, 29.444731 m, and 92.326073 m. These
explore surface, shallow, and intermediate currents. They do not imply a known A76C draft, and no
vertical interpolation or invented depth is used.

Three independently restartable depth chunks cover 2 January through 27 August 2026. Every file
contains 949 times, 126 latitudes, 133 longitudes, one depth, `uo`, and `vo`. Raw NetCDF and its
checksum sidecars remain Git-ignored.

## ERA5 wind authentication gate

The official Climate Data Store dataset is `reanalysis-era5-single-levels`, ERA5 hourly data on
single levels. The live catalogue verifies `10m_u_component_of_wind` and
`10m_v_component_of_wind`, both in `m s-1`. ERA5—not ERA5-Land—is required over the ocean.

No `.cdsapirc` was configured. Wind files and values therefore remain absent and explicitly flagged
`ERA5_AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED`. The user must sign in at the Climate Data Store, accept the ERA5
licence, copy the personal API token configuration shown by CDS into `%USERPROFILE%\.cdsapirc`, and
then rerun acquisition. No credential value is stored or printed by POLARIS-AI.

## Sampling and coverage

USNIC supplies only a date for each position. The original date precision is preserved; sampling at
00 UTC is an explicit alignment convention and every sample carries `USNIC_DATE_PRECISION_ONLY`.
The sampler performs bilinear interpolation from four regular-grid neighbours and linear
interpolation between bracketing six-hour timestamps. It never extrapolates: requests outside space
or time are returned missing with a quality flag. A missing source corner makes the sample missing
rather than inventing a value.

All 105 point/depth combinations were covered. Surface interval summaries use a six-hour sequence
along the WGS 84 geodesic between consecutive observed positions: 982 of 982 expected ocean samples
were valid across 34 intervals. This is labelled `TRACK-ALIGNED ENVIRONMENTAL SUMMARY`; it is a
simple observed-path approximation, not an exact reconstruction of all forcing on the iceberg.

The processed NetCDF retains track coordinates, source depth, ocean components, deliberately missing
wind fields, quality flags, classifications, interpolation methods, and raw-source provenance.

## Initial diagnostics and limitations

Across the 105 position/depth samples, current speed averaged 0.1326 m/s and ranged from 0.0120 to
0.3527 m/s. Surface interval east-component correlation with observed eastward iceberg velocity was
0.5002; north-component correlation was 0.4937. Mean vector alignment cosine was 0.5124 across 33
non-zero-displacement intervals (median 0.9208). These are exploratory associations, not causal
coefficients.

The existing OSI-SAF file covers Bharati/Prydz Bay, not A76C's -38.5 to -29.5 longitude track, so
sea-ice concentration is `not covered`; it is not replaced with zero or fabricated values.

No 2% wind rule, draft, thickness, mass, force balance, drag coefficient, Coriolis integration,
uncertainty model, or trajectory prediction is implemented. Large tabular icebergs can be dominated
by ocean currents, and wind coefficients will not be chosen before verified wind data exist.
